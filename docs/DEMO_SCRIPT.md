# 60-second demo script

**0:00 — The desk.** The Election Desk opens: a globe, with dispatch lines from Novi Sad.
> "We built this here in Novi Sad. Three elections in ten days: Serbia, the US and South Africa."

**0:08 — Serbia dossier.** Point at the OSCE quote about media bias.
> "Observers said bias and online manipulation hampered Serbian voters' access to reliable information. Every line here is a verbatim quote, linked to its source."

**0:16 — The test claim.** Point at the *Synthetic test claim* label.
> "We wrote this claim to test the court. It uses the observers' real words about high turnout, and leaves out the rest. Let's put it on trial." Click **Open the investigation**.

**0:22 — CASE OPENED.** The stamp lands; three agents light up.
> "One event, three Mozaik agents, three independent loops, all starting at the same instant. The timeline is drawn from Mozaik's own events."

**0:30 — Evidence.** OSCE quotes drop into the ledger.
> "Every quote is checked word for word against the OSCE report. Paraphrases are rejected."

**0:38 — Cross-examination.** Challenge arrows appear, tagged *Framing*.
> "They challenge each other, and the court tracks whether a dispute is about facts or about framing."

**0:46 — Verdict.** The front page prints **MISLEADING**.
> "Misleading. Not false: the words are real. The problem is selective quotation, and the court says so, separately from the facts, along with what is still uncertain."

**0:54 — Close.** Highlight a sentence on any site, right-click, **Put This On Trial**.
> "The Ground Truth. Don't just read the news. Cross-examine it."
